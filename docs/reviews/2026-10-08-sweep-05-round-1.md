# Sweep 05a, dialectical round one, partial (Claude against the apparatus, 2026-10-08, on three cells of nine; the rest in the next session)

The design after round one against sweep 04: two layers, the scrambled-pairs control read beside every meaning curve, the plateau rule on a held-out set, the identification excess beside the error, every point placed by its distance from the training table and the moved table's own entropy; the remap knob off. Nine cells as nine processes; three had finished at the close of the session that launched them. Numbers stay in the log. This round is against what the three can show.

1. **The model as trained did not read the pairs.** The scrambled-pairs control reads zero to the digit in every cell, at every rank, on the same table and at every radius: permuting the earlier extensions of a window changes the loss at the later separators by nothing. Whatever two layers make possible, this training did not reach it. The control did its first job: where the meaning loss drops after the first artifact (one cell at the free walk), the drop survives scrambling, so it is positional, not the pairs being read, which closes the suspicion of the previous round's item 2 for these cells.

2. **The plateau rule ended training after two or three held-out readings.** A hundred to a hundred and fifty steps, the held-out loss still high and in one cell rising between the second and third reading: one non-improving reading was enough to stop. Noise in a held-out set of sixteen contexts at this learning rate is above the one-percent tolerance, so the rule fires at the first flat or rising pair. Fixed in code the same hour: the rule now takes a patience (consecutive non-improving readings) and a minimum step count; the next run sets both. Until the model is trained to a real plateau, item 1 says nothing about two layers and everything about the training.

3. **The identification excess sat slightly below zero in every reading.** The floor was the standard error of a frequency; the expected absolute error is √(2/π) of it, and the shortfall was that factor. Fixed in code the same hour; the three cells' excess is read as zero at the floor, which is the count's √k line holding on the same table once more.

4. **The two coordinates are now in every point** (distance from the training table; the moved table's entropy), with the free walk again at the edge of the plane: in one table a dying language (entropy zero, every artifact one form), in another a higher-entropy table than the training one. Nothing is read against them yet.

Verdict on the three cells: nothing survives; the two-layer model was not trained long enough for the control to say whether it can read a pairing, and the rule that cut the training short is corrected. The six remaining cells are read in the next session for the same items; the next run trains with patience and a minimum, and only then is the scramble cost a reading on the architecture.

## Addendum: the other six cells (landed at 14:04, before the three-hour mark)

5. **Two cells died in the arithmetic.** The composed softmax cross-entropy exponentiated raw scores; with two layers at this learning rate the logits of two cells grew past a float's range and training ended in an overflow in `exp` and a domain error in `log`. Fixed in code the same hour: the scores are shifted by their maximum before `exp`, a constant that leaves the softmax and its gradient unchanged; a test with scores a thousand apart. The two cells are rerun with the next run.

6. **The learning rate is too high for two layers.** In one of the cells that finished, the held-out loss more than doubled between its last two readings, which is the same instability short of overflow; it also made the plateau rule fire, since a rising reading counts as not improving. The run script now takes the learning rate, the patience and the minimum as arguments; the next run lowers the rate.

7. **The control stayed at zero in all seven cells that finished**, including the three that trained to two or three hundred steps: within this training the two-layer model read nothing from the pairs. The induction circuit is known to form late in training, after a phase change (Olsson et al. 2022; from training, marked), so the cap and the patience must give it room before the control's zero is read as a fact about the architecture rather than the budget.

Verdict after seven cells: unchanged; nothing survives. The next run: the shifted loss, a lower learning rate, patience and a minimum in the plateau rule, the cap raised, and only then is the scramble cost a reading on two layers.

## Before 05b lands: the adequate size, derived from the counts (2026-10-08, 15:11)

Izzy: the adequate size should be derivable, conceptually, and at least empirically to an extent. The derivation, in the apparatus's own counts, lodged before 05b's cells finish.

Reading a pair is a match and a copy. The match is a cut over the strings in play: at the separator of the query string the model must find the earlier separator whose string is the same, which means keeping every pair of distinct strings apart. The rank that cut needs is the table's effective string count. At 05b's size (forms 4, nonterminals 3, resolution 4, table seed 1) the table produces 24 distinct strings, 3.65 bits, 12.6 effective, of length one to five. The model supplies rank in two ways: width, the dimension of the key space the match is scored in, and layers, how many tokens of a string one position can see, since the induction circuit hands a position its predecessor one layer at a time. At width 4 no set of 24 keys is near-orthogonal, so the softmax match carries a structural ambiguity floor whatever the training does; and two layers see at most the last two tokens of a string up to five long, so what can form is a match on a suffix class, not on the string. The copy is the easy half.

The budget is a third count. The circuit forms when the bits the pairs save over the marginal, summed over training, pay for the circuit's own description: parameters at the weight's grain. The pairs' saving per window is the meaning floor's gap that the identification excess reads; the circuit's cost is its parameter count (1456 scalars at 05b's size) times the mantissa. That crossing is the field's phase transition in data stated in our counts, and it is a number the apparatus can compute once the width is adequate.

**Prediction, before the numbers:** 05b's scrambled-pairs control at depth zero stays at zero or near it, whatever the learning rate did, because the full match cannot form at width 4 with two layers. If it reads clearly non-zero, a match on the last two tokens suffices for this table's extensions, and the derived floor is the suffix classes' count (at most sixteen), not the strings'.

**The empirical side ("you're not blind"):** two calibration cells launched at 15:11 on the spare cores, drift off (radius 0, depth 8), width 8 at two and at four layers, the rest as 05b (`runs/sweep5.py calib-w8L2 4 3 4 8 40 100 8 0 0 1 1 3000 2 0.1 3 300`, and `calib-w8L4 ... 3000 4 ...`). The control at depth zero against width and layers is the measured floor; the derived one above is what it is compared with. Width 16 and beyond cost sixteen times a width-4 cell in pure Python; that is where a numpy model becomes the question (the README reserves it for Izzy).

## Round one against 05b (2026-10-08, 20:30; nine cells, learning rate 0.1, patience 3, minimum 300, cap 3000; numbers in the logs under the rule)

The run as it happened: eight cells ended on the held-out plateau rule between a thousand and two thousand steps, one ran to the cap; three to five and a half hours each on the scalar engine (about 82 minutes of readings plus 5.6 seconds per training step at this size, fitted on two cells and checked on three; my "an hour per cell" before the launch was 05a's wall time taken as the cost of a cell, which it was not). Every number below is a reading of a corner: training on the stationary table (decisions 28), the sum-of-cuts extension token (17), one table per window, single-cut leaves, the generator's truth as the distance axis (9).

1. **The derivation's prediction held.** The scrambled-pairs control at depth zero reads at or near zero in all nine cells. The one cell that ran to the cap shows a small positive cost at the middle ranks, the only trace in the sweep that the pairs were read at all: the budget moves the control at the margin, as the crossing says it should, and the width-and-layers floor bounds what the budget can buy. Nothing here bears on either prediction. The instrument at width 4 with two layers cannot form the match; the derived size stands.
2. **The meaning channel is string-blind, not only pair-blind.** The meaning excess at depth zero sits well above a floor of zero: the extension is a function of the string in these tables, and the model does not read a string's own extension within the table it was trained on. That is the same floor seen from the other side: reading the extension needs the string's identity, which width 4 cannot hold for twenty-four strings.
3. **Drift readings say nothing yet.** Under drift the meaning excess rises with distance and the curves do not fall with rank: no in-context recovery, at a size that cannot recover and under training that held no drift. Uninformative about both predictions by construction.
4. **The probe varies by table.** The linear probe from the last separator's attended vector to the drift magnitude reads something in one table's cells and nothing in the others'. Unexplained; a replication by size would be needed before it is a reading, and it is not one now.
5. **The readers behaved.** The identification excess is at its sampling floor everywhere with full coverage, and the plateau rule with patience ended training where the held-out loss had settled; the budget objection from 05a is closed at this size.

Verdict: nothing survives; no number is spoken; the instrument is confirmed below its derived floor, which was the only thing this run could confirm. Next: no run below the derived size, and no run of a corner: the complete system (the shuffled ball in training, the mixture of tables and states within a window, the weighted leaf, the distance read from the ball, the artifacts' estimate as the axis, both drift sources), built once on numpy at the derived size and read once. The calibration cells at width 8 (old token, drift off) keep running as the one instrument check still worth having before the port.
