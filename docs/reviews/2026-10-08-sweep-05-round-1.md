# Sweep 05a, dialectical round one, partial (Claude against the apparatus, 2026-10-08, on three cells of nine; the rest in the next session)

The design after round one against sweep 04: two layers, the scrambled-pairs control read beside every meaning curve, the plateau rule on a held-out set, the identification excess beside the error, every point placed by its distance from the training table and the moved table's own entropy; the remap knob off. Nine cells as nine processes; three had finished at the close of the session that launched them. Numbers stay in the log. This round is against what the three can show.

1. **The model as trained did not read the pairs.** The scrambled-pairs control reads zero to the digit in every cell, at every rank, on the same table and at every radius: permuting the earlier extensions of a window changes the loss at the later separators by nothing. Whatever two layers make possible, this training did not reach it. The control did its first job: where the meaning loss drops after the first artifact (one cell at the free walk), the drop survives scrambling, so it is positional, not the pairs being read, which closes the suspicion of the previous round's item 2 for these cells.

2. **The plateau rule ended training after two or three held-out readings.** A hundred to a hundred and fifty steps, the held-out loss still high and in one cell rising between the second and third reading: one non-improving reading was enough to stop. Noise in a held-out set of sixteen contexts at this learning rate is above the one-percent tolerance, so the rule fires at the first flat or rising pair. Fixed in code the same hour: the rule now takes a patience (consecutive non-improving readings) and a minimum step count; the next run sets both. Until the model is trained to a real plateau, item 1 says nothing about two layers and everything about the training.

3. **The identification excess sat slightly below zero in every reading.** The floor was the standard error of a frequency; the expected absolute error is √(2/π) of it, and the shortfall was that factor. Fixed in code the same hour; the three cells' excess is read as zero at the floor, which is the count's √k line holding on the same table once more.

4. **The two coordinates are now in every point** (distance from the training table; the moved table's entropy), with the free walk again at the edge of the plane: in one table a dying language (entropy zero, every artifact one form), in another a higher-entropy table than the training one. Nothing is read against them yet.

Verdict on the three cells: nothing survives; the two-layer model was not trained long enough for the control to say whether it can read a pairing, and the rule that cut the training short is corrected. The six remaining cells are read in the next session for the same items; the next run trains with patience and a minimum, and only then is the scramble cost a reading on the architecture.
