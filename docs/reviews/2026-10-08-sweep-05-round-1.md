The design after round one against sweep 04: two layers, the scrambled-pairs control read beside every meaning curve, the plateau rule on a held-out set, the identification excess beside the error, every point placed by its distance from the training table and the moved table's own entropy; the remap knob off. 1. **The model as trained did not read the pairs.** The scrambled-pairs control reads zero to the digit in every cell, at every rank, on the same table and at every radius: permuting the earlier extensions of a window changes the loss at the later separators by nothing. Whatever two layers make possible, this training did not reach it. The control did its first job: where the meaning loss drops after the first artifact (one cell at the free walk), the drop survives scrambling, so it is positional, not the pairs being read, which closes the suspicion of the previous round's item 2 for these cells.

2. **The plateau rule ended training after two or three held-out readings.** A hundred to a hundred and fifty steps, the held-out loss still high and in one cell rising between the second and third reading: one non-improving reading was enough to stop. Noise in a held-out set of sixteen contexts at this learning rate is above the one-percent tolerance, so the rule fires at the first flat or rising pair. Fixed in code the same hour: the rule now takes a patience (consecutive non-improving readings) and a minimum step count; the next run sets both. Until the model is trained to a real plateau, item 1 says nothing about two layers and everything about the training.

3. **The identification excess sat slightly below zero in every reading.** The floor was the standard error of a frequency; the expected absolute error is √(2/π) of it, and the shortfall was that factor. ## Addendum: the other six cells (landed at 14:04, before the three-hour mark)

5. **Two cells died in the arithmetic.** The composed softmax cross-entropy exponentiated raw scores; with two layers at this learning rate the logits of two cells grew past a float's range and training ended in an overflow in `exp` and a domain error in `log`. Fixed in code the same hour: the scores are shifted by their maximum before `exp`, a constant that leaves the softmax and its gradient unchanged; a test with scores a thousand apart. The two cells are rerun with the next run.

6. **The learning rate is too high for two layers.** In one of the cells that finished, the held-out loss more than doubled between its last two readings, which is the same instability short of overflow; it also made the plateau rule fire, since a rising reading counts as not improving. The run script now takes the learning rate, the patience and the minimum as arguments; the next run lowers the rate.

7. **The control stayed at zero in all seven cells that finished**, including the three that trained to two or three hundred steps: within this training the two-layer model read nothing from the pairs. The induction circuit is known to form late in training, after a phase change (Olsson et al. 2022; from training, marked), so the cap and the patience must give it room before the control's zero is read as a fact about the architecture rather than the budget.

Verdict after seven cells: unchanged; nothing survives. The next run: the shifted loss, a lower learning rate, patience and a minimum in the plateau rule, the cap raised, and only then is the scramble cost a reading on two layers.

## Before 05b lands: the adequate size, derived from the counts (2026-10-08, 15:25)

Izzy: the adequate size should be derivable, conceptually, and at least empirically to an extent. The derivation, in the apparatus's own counts, lodged before 05b's cells finish.

Reading a pair is a match and a copy. The match is a cut over the strings in play: at the separator of the query string the model must find the earlier separator whose string is the same, which means keeping every pair of distinct strings apart. The rank that cut needs is the table's effective string count. At 05b's size (forms 4, nonterminals 3, resolution 4, table seed 1) the table produces 24 distinct strings, 3.65 bits, 12.6 effective, of length one to five. The model supplies rank in two ways: width, the dimension of the key space the match is scored in, and layers, how many tokens of a string one position can see, since the induction circuit hands a position its predecessor one layer at a time. At width 4 no set of 24 keys is near-orthogonal, so the softmax match carries a structural ambiguity floor whatever the training does; and two layers see at most the last two tokens of a string up to five long, so what can form is a match on a suffix class, not on the string. The copy is the easy half.

The budget is a third count. The circuit forms when the bits the pairs save over the marginal, summed over training, pay for the circuit's own description: parameters at the weight's grain. The pairs' saving per window is the meaning floor's gap that the identification excess reads; the circuit's cost is its parameter count (1456 scalars at 05b's size) times the mantissa. That crossing is the field's phase transition in data stated in our counts, and it is a number the apparatus can compute once the width is adequate.

**Prediction, before the numbers:** 05b's scrambled-pairs control at depth zero stays at zero or near it, whatever the learning rate did, because the full match cannot form at width 4 with two layers. If it reads clearly non-zero, a match on the last two tokens suffices for this table's extensions, and the derived floor is the suffix classes' count (at most sixteen), not the strings'.
