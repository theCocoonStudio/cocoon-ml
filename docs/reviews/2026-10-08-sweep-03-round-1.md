# Sweeps 03a, 03b, 03c, 03d, dialectical round one (Claude against the apparatus, 2026-10-08)

Four sweeps on the apparatus after the fixes from round one against sweeps 02: a the base size, b a larger table and context, c a wider model on a's drift (the width control), d the base size on a second drift stream (the second replication of the source). Nine cells each, six depths, a hundred windows per depth, three models per table reading one drift walk. a and d finished first and are read here; b and c are read in the addendum. Numbers stay in the log. This round is against what the sweeps can show.

1. **Depth is not distance under reversion.** With the pull toward the origin the index reaches its stationary band within a few steps and wanders inside it, so the distance from the training table does not grow with depth: in both a and d the measured delta rises and falls across the depths, and the six depths are six draws from one band, not a trajectory. Every curve against depth is a curve against nothing. The step is not wrong; the axis was. Fix: the x-axis is the measured distance (`delta_magnitude`, or the identification distance between the two tables, both exact), and the knob that sets it is the radius, which is consistency itself: the next design sweeps the radius at a fixed depth past the mixing time, not the depth at a fixed radius.

2. **The input never carries an extension, so meaning cannot be learned in context.** `pack_meaning` puts an artifact's extension at its separator as the target; the token sequence the model reads holds forms and separators only. Nothing in a window tells the model what any earlier artifact meant, so the meaning loss at the k-th separator is a lookup trained into the weights, and its dependence on k can only come through the forms. The meaning curve is not an in-context readout as built, and the predictions, which separate on the recovery of a remapped pair, cannot be reached by it. Fix: pairs in the input. Each artifact's extension token follows its separator as a token; the target at a separator stays the extension; the loss at the k-th separator is then the extension of the k-th string given k − 1 (string, extension) pairs in context, the setting the predictions are about.

3. **The incidence never moves, so there is no I_Δ to recover.** The step moves shares, orders and phases, which strings occur; the form–cut pairs are fixed by `generate` and nothing in `step` touches them. The design of 2026-10-08 afternoon chose form drift with meaning fixed, and the apparatus is faithful to it; but the count puts the disagreement between the two predictions at I_Δ, pairs the training incidence did not hold, and no run on this apparatus produces one. Both predictions are untested by construction. Fix: a second, separate move on the incidence (a leaf's cut remapped), the protocol axis of the page's test ("remap the meaning of one morpheme"), as its own knob beside the form walk, so that the plane the two axes span is what is swept. A check from the floors: in the six tables of a, b and d no string carries two extensions (the meaning floor is exactly zero), so meaning here is a function of the string; whether that is the schema or an accident of `generate` at these counts is a question for the page, since synonymy and ambiguity of meaning are in the definitions and absent from the source.

4. **The window is too short to identify anything, by the count's own arithmetic.** At this context length a window holds a few artifacts, and the count pins a share to about √(kπ) levels of ρ: at these k that is one or two levels of eight, and the identification error reads exactly that: at depth zero it sits at the sampling floor of a handful of draws, and under drift it hardly moves, because the window cannot tell the moved table from the training table any better than it can tell either from noise. The curves of this sweep measure nothing the window could have identified. Fix: lower the resolution (ρ of two or four, so ρ² artifacts fit a window) and lengthen the context to hold them, which the scalar autograd can afford at those sizes; k must reach the count's threshold or the run tests a bound nobody disputes.

5. **The form curve rises with rank at depth zero, in seven cells of nine in each of a and d.** Contexts fill now, so the training distribution of positions is not the cause. The model has absolute positions and one layer: at rank zero the absolute position is the position inside the artifact, later it is not, and next-form prediction inside an artifact needs the latter. The rise is the model failing to locate itself, an artefact of the architecture, not drift. Checked by subtraction from the log: the drifted excess minus the depth-zero excess, rank by rank, has no consistent sign across depths in either sweep; the rise cancels and what remains is the size of the noise. Fix: a position-since-the-last-separator embedding beside the absolute one; and recovery reported as the drifted curve minus the same-table curve in the script, which the sweep-02 round asked for and this sweep's script did not do.

6. **Training is not at a plateau.** The loss is still falling between the middle and the end of training in every cell of a and d, and models of one table differ by an order of magnitude in meaning excess at depth zero. The sweep-02 round asked for training to a plateau; the script trained a fixed number of steps. Fix: a stopping rule (the mean loss over a window of steps improving by less than a set fraction), with a cap, reported with the cell.

7. **n_eff equals foreignness in nearly every cell.** Under the bounded walk no share reaches zero, so every reading remains possible under both tables, and n_eff, which counts readings, cannot see the walk; it differed from foreignness only where the lean had pushed a share to an end, which the pull now prevents. As designed it is the coverage term and nothing more; the identification error is the only reader of a move in this sweep. Both are kept, with that stated.

8. **The probe's target has no variance beyond sampling.** The drift a window shows against the training table is, at this k, the same sampling noise as the identification error against the moved table, so the probe is asked to read noise off the activations; the held-out r² ranges from nothing to moderate across seeds with no pattern, the signature of sample size, as in sweep 01. The fix follows from item 4: longer windows, and the target then has a signal to carry.

9. **What the replication established.** d is a's tables and models on a second drift stream. Its depth-zero rows are identical to a's to the last digit, which is what determinism of the generator, the training and the windows must give, and is a check that passed; its drifted rows differ from a's, so the second stream is a second draw of the source, and the distances it draws differ from a's in both directions, as item 1 says they must.

Verdict on a and d: the apparatus runs without freezing, and the fixed step exposed the axis: depth means nothing under reversion. Two readouts measure nothing the predictions are about: the meaning curve has no in-context channel, and the incidence never moves. The windows are below the count's own threshold. Nothing from these numbers survives to be spoken; no result exists for the rule to apply to. The next design: pairs in the input; a move on the incidence as the second axis; the radius swept as the first; ρ lowered and contexts lengthened past the threshold; a within-artifact position; training to a plateau; the same-table curve subtracted in the script.

## Addendum: b and c (the same day, when they ended)

10. **c, the width control.** The same tables, the same drift stream and the same windows as a: every reader of the source (delta, identification error, n_eff, artifacts per window) is identical to a's to the digit, as it must be, so c differs from a in the model alone. The wider model trains lower, its form excess is lower at every depth by about the same amount, and its drifted-minus-same differences are noise as in a; the rise with rank survives the width. What the control shows: the excess in a is the model's capacity before it is anything about drift, and item 5's cause is the position, not the width.

11. **b, the larger table and context.** Where the strings are short a window holds two to three times the artifacts, and there the identification error sits lower and the probe reads more than elsewhere in the sweep; that is the direction of item 4, k against ρ², confounded with everything else that table is, and not a result. And a degenerate stretch: one table's index spends its two deepest depths at the end of a share, where the table produces one one-form string: its form floor exactly zero, every window the same number of artifacts, a quarter of the nodes identified at zero error. Not a freeze (the delta differs between the two depths; the walk moves), but a language at the end of the dying direction inside its allowed band, because the origin sat near the end and a band of two grains around it includes the end. Two things follow. The moved table's own entropy, its floors, is the resolution axis of the page, and it must be reported beside the distance, the consistency axis, as the second coordinate of every point: the script measures both and the curves used neither as an axis. And a table whose origin sits near an end has a band that reaches the end, which the radius does not prevent and the page should say when it says the walk is bounded.

12. **The plateau, in b and c.** Still falling between the middle and the end of training in seven cells of nine in b and nine of nine in c, by the same test as item 6.

Verdict after b and c: unchanged. Nothing from the four sweeps survives to be spoken. b adds the second coordinate to the next design (every point placed by the moved table's entropy and its distance from the training table), and c places the model's capacity above the drift in the excess as read at this k.
