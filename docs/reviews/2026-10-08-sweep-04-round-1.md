# Sweeps 04a and 04b, dialectical round one (Claude against the apparatus, 2026-10-08)

The design after round one against sweeps 03: pairs in the input, the within-artifact position, training to a plateau rule, the resolution lowered to four and the context lengthened past the count's identification threshold, the radius swept (none, one grain, two grains) at a fixed depth past mixing with the measured distance as the x-axis, the remap knob off (the design as Izzy set it), the same-table curves subtracted in the script. 04a: the base size, nine cells as nine processes, a hundred windows per condition. 04b: a larger table and context, four cells, read in the addendum when it ends. Numbers stay in the log. This round is against what the sweeps can show.

1. **The model is below the minimum circuit for what the pairs ask.** With pairs in the input, recovering the extension of the k-th string from the earlier pairs means matching the string against earlier strings and reading the token that followed the match: a conditional copy. One layer of attention cannot do it: a single head conditions its attention on the current position's query against the keys of earlier positions, and can gather earlier extension tokens, but never "the token after the position that matched", which takes a second layer (the induction circuit: Elhage et al. 2021, Olsson et al. 2022; from training, marked). What one layer can read from the pairs is the in-context marginal of extensions, a bag of earlier extension tokens. So the meaning curves of this sweep, flat across ranks under drift in every cell and of both signs against the same-table curve where the radius is bounded, are what the architecture predicts before any data, not what the data say about the bound. Fix: two layers, the minimal model that can read a pairing, on the scalar autograd (the cost doubles; a cell at this size took forty minutes to an hour, so it stays feasible). Until then no meaning curve from this apparatus bears on either prediction.

2. **No scrambled-pairs control.** On the same table the meaning excess at the first separator sits above the later ranks in some cells and not in others. A drop after the first artifact could be the pairs being read, the first position being special under the within-artifact embedding, or the padding of the first context slot; nothing in the sweep separates them. Fix: the same contexts with the earlier extension tokens scrambled among themselves, as a control condition read beside every curve. If the curve does not rise under scrambling, the pairs were not read, whatever else moved.

3. **The plateau rule stopped at the noise floor of the batch loss.** Training ended by the rule in every cell, well under the cap, and the loss between the middle and the end of training is still falling in eight cells of nine by the same test as before. A one-percent improvement over fifty steps on batches of four contexts is the batch noise, not a plateau. Fix: the stopping statistic read on a fixed held-out set of contexts every fifty steps, with a longer window; and the cell reports the last two readings so the reviewer can see the slope.

4. **The identification error is not comparable across tables of different share entropy.** A share at an end of its range is identified from a handful of draws with no error; the tables reached by the free walk sit at the ends, so their identification error is lower than the training table's own, which reads as "better identified under drift" and means "lower entropy". Fix: the error over its sampling floor, per node, from the true share and the visit count (the standard error of a frequency), summed as a ratio of counts: the identification excess. The same-table reading of this sweep is that floor itself, which is a check the count passes: a share near one half, visited about nine times, is pinned to about the grain, as √(kπ) against ρ says.

5. **The free walk is not one condition, and the page's direction is not what it does.** At radius none the index is wherever a reflecting walk of sixty-four steps happens to be: in one table a dying language (the form floor exactly zero, every artifact one form, a third of the nodes never visited), in another a table of higher entropy than the training table. The page says that left alone resolution falls; the walk as built, with this lean against reflection at a resolution of four, went both ways. Two things follow: the dying direction needs its own measure, the moved table's floor as the resolution coordinate (item 11 of the previous round), and its own test; and "radius none" is a draw from a long walk, not a point on the axis. The x-axis of every point is the pair (distance from the training table, the moved table's entropy); the script has both and plotted neither.

6. **The window now carries the drift.** The drift a window shows against the training table rises with the radius, above the same-table sampling floor, in every cell: the windows are long enough for the first time that the probe's target has variance beyond sampling. The probe's held-out reading remains seed-dependent across the nine cells, and nothing is read from it before item 1 is closed; with one layer the activations at the separator can hold the bag of earlier extensions and little else.

7. **Form does not recover either.** The drifted-minus-same form excess does not fall with rank in any cell: the model does not adapt its next-form prediction to the window at any radius. Consistent with item 1: one layer reads skip-trigram statistics, and the form statistics of a window are a longer-range object. Form recovery is a readout for the two-layer model as well.

8. **The second axis is still fixed.** The remap knob stayed off, as the design has it; this sweep swept consistency alone and could not have reached I_Δ. The axis waits for Izzy's word, and when it turns on, the scrambled-pairs control of item 2 is what shows whether a remapped pair is read at all.

9. **Replication.** 04b, the larger table and context, is read in the addendum. There is no identity check between a and b (different tables), so b replicates the shape of the findings, not the numbers; the width control of sweep 03 is not repeated here.

Verdict on 04a: the apparatus now has the in-context channel the test needs, and the readers of the source (coverage, the drift shown, the floors) work as designed; the model under the channel is below the minimum circuit for reading it, so no curve of this sweep bears on either prediction, and nothing from its numbers survives to be spoken. The next design: two layers; the scrambled-pairs control; the identification excess over the sampling floor; the plateau rule on a held-out set; every point placed by distance and entropy; the remap axis at Izzy's word.
