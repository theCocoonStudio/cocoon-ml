## The cells

- The world and the model of decisions 55 and `runs/launch-08.sh`: the Markov world, four states at resolution 8, Dirichlet 1 with end 1, two chains, corpus 42 000 artifacts, windows of 64 tokens; the array model at (96, 7), the derived scale, rate 0.05, mode cap to 2 488 steps with the three copies; seed 1.
- Two cells of 2026-10-11 reused through their saved copies: `sweep-08-s0-w96-lr0.05` (no steps: the training walk stationary at the origins, radius none) and `sweep-08-s1-r1-w96-lr0.05` (steps 1, training radius 1 grain).
- Two cells launched 2026-10-18 23:18:08 UTC (`~/.claude/scratch/runs/launch-08b.sh`, from the archive of 6416d32, the head of main): `sweep-08b-s1-r3-w96-lr0.05` and `sweep-08b-s1-r6-w96-lr0.05`, training radii 3 and 6 grains with steps 1, read radii 0, 1, 3, 6 and free. The first cells trained under drift wider than one grain.
- The instrument, `runs/reference8.py` (this branch): for a cell and a copy, the reading corpora regenerated from sweep8's seeds, the model's form loss by rank, the Bayes floor under the reading law (exact at radius 0, the particle filter with 200 particles elsewhere) and the marginal reader (the reading windows' counts) by rank on the first 64 windows of each reading, and S by rank; the record's cut at the rank every window reaches, and a deep curve past it with counts. Run on the best copy of every cell and the marginal copy of the radius-1 cell (`launch-ref8-a.sh`, then the new cells' copies).

## The choices (listed before the reading, the owner's protocol)

1. The world: the Markov world rather than the schema's, because its references exist (decisions 54, 55) and S needs both.
2. The training radii 3 and 6 grains beside the record's 0 and 1: the bound of the walk is five sixteenths per step at this world, so 6 grains is three quarters of the share scale, the wide end; 3 is between. Nothing derives these two numbers.
3. Steps 1 in training for both new cells, as the radius-1 cell: the radius is the one knob moved.
4. The reading radii 0, 1, 3, 6, free for the new cells (the record's cells read 0, 1, 2, 3, free): the training radii themselves plus the free walk, so each cell reads at its own radius and beyond.
5. 64 windows per reading for the references (about 3.5 s a window for the floor at 200 particles, measured 23:0x): the first 64 of each reading corpus, not a draw.
6. The marginal reader of S is the reading windows' own counts (4.3's R0 under the reading law); the training windows' counts are recorded beside it.
7. The deep curve's rule: no cut, the count of positions per rank recorded; the record's rule (ranks every window reaches, sweep 02 round one) is kept as the main curve. At length 64 in this world every window reaches two whole artifacts, so the main curve has two ranks; the k axis the fork needs is longer than this window gives (a design fact for the row: a longer window or a shorter artifact).
8. One seed. A pilot.

## What each outcome shows (written before the reading)

- If a new cell escapes or sits on the marginal to the cap: training under wide drift fails at this size or rate; the row needs the rate rule rerun at these radii before anything else.
- S at the free reading (the farthest), best copy, by training radius 0, 1, 3, 6: the fork's first look. Falling with the training radius is the direction of the owner's prediction (recovery past the training radius rises with r_train) and of Lemma 5's corollary (kappa_v falling with rho_t); flat within the spread is the degenerate case; rising is neither and a finding against both.
- S at the training radius itself across the four cells, against S at the free reading: the gap between inside and outside the ball per cell; a gap that shrinks with the training radius is the pull washing out.
- The marginal copy against the best copy at radius 1: whether S moved with training at all (the instrument's sanity).
- The ball's cardinality per cell (`ball_states`): the axis of Raventós et al. (literature entry 13); whether the four cells sit on one side or both of a threshold is read off these counts; at radius 1 the record holds 21 729 distinct states of 42 000 artifacts, so this world is at the high-diversity end in their terms and the radius, not the count, is the axis here.
- None of these is a result: one seed, one size, no null written; the row's form (decision 53) comes after the owner's strike.

## What the instrument read (23:50 UTC; the cells' own lines, the best copy; S at a distance pending)

**The cells.** Both new cells ran to the cap of 2 488 steps with no escape: r3's onset at step 950, best held loss 1.424 at step 2 150; r6's onset at 500, best 1.433 at 2 050 (the record's r1: onset 1 300, best 1.437 at 2 450; s0: onset 850, best 1.389 at 2 100). The marginal copies sit at 1.61 to 1.63, the in-cell references at the training radius (sixteen held windows) at marginal 1.59 to 1.60 and floor 1.28 (r1), 1.30 (r3), 1.39 (r6), 1.23 (s0, exact): the floor rises with the training radius, as a wider walk makes the held windows less predictable. The ball: 2 states (s0), 21 729 (r1), 40 645 (r3), 40 661 (r6) of 42 000 artifacts: at radii 3 and 6 nearly every artifact's state is distinct, so in this world the training radius widens the ball's spread and not its cardinality past radius 1; the axis of literature entry 13 (the number of distinct tasks) is saturated here and the radius is the axis, as choice 8 of the design said.

**The model's form loss by rank at each reading radius (the cells' `points`, best copy; rank 1 then rank 2 where every window reaches it; the standard error about 0.015 at rank 1 and 0.02 to 0.03 at rank 2).** Inside the ball (read radius 0, truth distance 0): s0 1.55, 1.51; r1 1.57, 1.56; r3 1.48, 1.52; r6 1.50, 1.54. At the matched distance of the radius-3 reading (truth distance 0.062 to 0.074): r1 1.58, 1.60; r3 1.49, 1.58; r6 1.51, 1.58. At the free reading, whose truth distance differs by cell since each cell's ball is its own (0.285 for r1, 0.228 for r3, 0.199 for r6): r1 1.62, 1.69; r3 1.59, 1.66; r6 1.55, 1.64. s0's readings are all at distance 0, since a cell without steps reads without steps at every radius (sweep8's rule), so it has no reading at a distance.

**Read against the outcome map.** (1) Training under drift at radii 3 and 6 works at this size and rate: no escape, an onset, a held loss below r1's. (2) At the farthest reading the loss falls with the training radius at both ranks, the direction of the owner's prediction and of Lemma 5's corollary; but two confounds sit in it: the free reading is not at one distance (the wider the ball, the nearer the same walk), and the cells differ inside the ball too (r1 is worse than r3 and r6 at distance 0 by about 0.09 at rank 1, six standard errors), so the far reading's order is partly the cells' levels. The rise from inside to the matched distance 0.06 to 0.07 is within one or two standard errors for every cell (r1 +0.01, r3 +0.01, r6 +0.01 at rank 1); the rise to the free reading is 0.05 (r1), 0.11 (r3), 0.05 (r6) at rank 1 and about 0.13 at rank 2 for all three, at different distances. So the pilot shows the far reading costing every cell something and the cost's order with the training radius not yet separable from the cells' levels and distances: the quantity the fork needs is S at a matched distance, the model's excess over the floor at that distance as a share of the marginal's, which removes the level and the law's own entropy from the comparison. (3) The k axis: `whole` is 1 or 2 at every drift reading, so the by-rank curve read the record's way has one or two points; the deep curve of reference8 (ranks past the cut, with counts) is the only look past them, with its selection.

**S, so far.** The instrument ran to completion on s0's best copy only (64 windows a reading, the floor exact): inside the ball S by rank is 0.83, 0.48, 0.50, 0.47, 0.66 at the stationary reading, that is the model leaves about half the resolvable bits unresolved at ranks 2 to 4 and most of them at rank 1, where the first artifact's state is unknown to it and partly known to the floor through the prior. The six other reference runs (r1 best and marginal; r3 and r6 best and marginal) did not finish: the first launch of three met the particle filter's refusal of a window on which every particle had zero likelihood (a far reading; the record's floor never met it because it ran at the training radius only), fixed as a retry at five times the particles and then a skip with counts; the relaunch and the second batch, seven processes in all beside nothing else, were stopped by the session's harness at about 23:50 UTC for the box's memory running low, with the instruction that they are not restarted on my own. One process at a time is the shape for the rerun (about fifteen minutes each at 64 windows); the measurement of one process's memory before any parallel launch is the rule learned, beside the rule that a launcher reads `free` first.

**The cause, found (04:35 UTC, the owner: "sounds like a leak"; a subagent's hunt with tracemalloc, one process at a time, on the scratch copy).** Not seven large processes: one process growing about 100 MB a window. `MarkovWorld` in `cocoonml/reference.py` memoises `_end_at`, `_end_within` and `_norm_of` by the chain's `id` and holds the chain in the value so the id stays valid; nothing is ever evicted, and under the particle filter every particle's chain is a fresh object each call, so no key repeats across windows and the memos only grow. tracemalloc after 4, 8 and 16 windows at radius 3 with 200 particles: the `_exact` memo 1.28 M, 2.56 M, 5.12 M entries (319 892 a window, two thirds of the growth: `_norm_of` recurses 400 lengths by 4 states for each of 200 particle chains at a window's first artifact), `_ends` 0.81 M, 1.31 M, 2.53 M, the chains held alive 6 791, 11 593, 22 549, the traced total 510, 867, 1 659 MB. Everything else flat; the ball's cache ends at 98 entries; the hypothesis lists free per window. The same world is shared across the sixteen held windows in `runs/sweep8.py`, so each training cell's floor carried about 1.6 GB too, bounded by sixteen. The fix (the same commit): `window_predictives` drops the memos when a sampled window is done, raised or not (`world.forget()` in a `finally`, on both worlds); the exact path's states are the origins, the same objects every window, so its memo repeats and is kept. Measured on the same command: resident memory 7 to 1 874 MB over 65 s before, 7 to 209 MB after, retained 59 MB after 4, 8 and 16 windows alike; the floors identical to the digit before and after; the suite's 210 tests pass. The six reference runs are relaunched two at a time from the fixed branch.

**What this pilot settles for the row, and what it does not.** Settled: the apparatus trains under drift to radius 6 grains at (96, 7); reference8 reads S at a distance once its runs complete; the training radius is the axis in this world, not the ball's cardinality; the window length bounds the k axis to two ranks and the row needs a longer window or a shorter artifact (`end_alpha`) if the asymptote in k is to be read; the far reading must be compared at matched truth distance, binned, not at the same nominal radius. Not settled: any direction of the fork; one seed, one size, no null; the owner's strike on the row stands before anything is spoken as a result.

**What this settles for the row, beyond the first reading.** The instrument reads S and its zero point is right; S at a distance must be read with its floor and marginal beside it, since the floor moves with the distance; the far readings are taken at bounded radii; the particle count for the floor is measured against the floor's own variance (two seeds of the filter on the same windows) before the row; and the training radius is the axis, with the three radii straddling what the fork needs decided by the owner.
