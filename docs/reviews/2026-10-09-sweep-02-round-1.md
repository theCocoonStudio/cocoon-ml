# Sweeps 02a, 02b, 02c, dialectical round one (Claude against the apparatus, 2026-10-09)

Three sweeps with the design after the first round: base size (02a), a larger table and context (02b), a wider model (02c); nine cells each; drift depths 0, 2, 4, 8, 16; a hundred windows per depth; twenty to forty-five minutes each. Numbers stay in the log. This round is against what they can show.

1. **The drift source dies.** In several cells the rows for depths 8 and 16, and in 02b for depths 2, 8 and 16, are identical to the last digit: the table stopped moving. The step clips shares to [0, 1] and the lean pushes toward the null sister, so shares reach an end and stay there; a share at zero is a remove and never returns. The index walks into an absorbing state within a few steps at this grain, which is not the diffusion the superposition derived: that one was mean-reverting with a bounded radius and never stopped. Fix: a reflecting or mean-reverting step, the Ornstein–Uhlenbeck form named on the page, so the index keeps moving. Until then every depth past the freeze is one depth, and the curves against depth are void.

2. **n_eff cannot see a moved share.** Where the table froze, n_eff against the moved table equals foreignness against the old one to the digit, which exposes the definition: n_eff counts visits and ambiguous visits, so two tables with the same readings and different shares read the same. It measures how much of the grammar a window touches, not how well the window pins the shares that moved. Fix: identification error, computable in the constructed source, the distance between the share estimated off the window's artifacts and the true share, per node; n_eff stays as the coverage term beside it.

3. **The form curve rises with rank.** In most cells the later artifacts in a context are predicted worse, not better, the opposite of recovery. The confound is training: contexts are packed from artifacts that fit, so later positions are rarer in training and their position embeddings undertrained; the curve reads the training distribution of positions. Fix: report recovery as the drifted curve minus the same-table curve at each rank, the floor subtracted, never the raw curve; and train on contexts that always fill.

4. **The last rank is a biased sample.** With whole artifacts only, the third or fourth artifact is present only when the earlier ones were short, so the last rank is read on a selected subset. Its jump in several cells is selection, not recovery. Fix: fixed artifact lengths, or report ranks only where the subset is the whole.

5. **Tables differ in entropy and the losses are compared raw.** Table 3 trains to a third of table 1's loss in every sweep because its distribution is sharper, not because the model learned more. Fix: subtract the table's true entropy, computable from `distribution`, which is the irreducible term of the information-theoretic decomposition the literature already has (2401.15530), and the first time that term is exact rather than estimated.

6. **The probe reads five levels.** The delta takes one value per depth, so the probe's target has five distinct values over hundreds of windows, and with the freeze fewer; the held-out r² is a fit to a near-constant target and says nothing about tracking. Fix: a continuous target, the per-window identification error or a per-window delta, so the probe has something to read.

7. **02a and 02c share their drift.** Same table seeds and the same random streams give the same drifted tables in both sweeps, which is the right control for the model's width and means the two are one replication of the source, not two.

Verdict: the second round of sweeps found the drift source broken and the readout blind to the thing under test. Three fixes in the design, the step, the identification error, the entropy floor, before any curve means anything. Nothing from these numbers survives to be spoken, and the rule's two rounds have not started on a result, because there is not one yet.
