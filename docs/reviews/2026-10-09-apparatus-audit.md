## The blind size derivation (Methuselah's second job, 2026-10-09; the author's derivation withheld from it), verified by Claude

Brief: from `attention.py` and `pack_pairs` alone, with the counts of sweep 05's table given (24 strings, 12.4 effective, lengths 1 to 5, suffix depth 4, five extension tokens at 2.03 bits a pair, about eight pairs a window), derive the floors for width, layers and the budget; the page's "The adequate size", the sweep 05 review, decisions 32 to 34 and the rule table withheld. (A first attempt that counted the derivations by hand overran its output; the counts were then given.)

| floor | Methuselah | the page (Claude, 2026-10-08) | verified |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| layers | **2**, under the assumption that one head gathers a recency-weighted bag of a string's last forms in one hop (the position embeddings giving a score linear in distance, the softmax a geometric profile); **5** under the reading that a head moves one position's content per hop (four gathers and the copy). Theorems from the code, all three held: at layer one a key is only its own token and position embeddings, so no key carries the string before it and a one-layer model reads at most the window's extension mix; the readout reads the last layer's attended vector only, so the copy is the last hop; by causality an earlier separator cannot hold its own extension, so the copy's source is the extension token after it, whose stream must have gathered its string in an earlier hop. | 5 (suffix depth plus the readout) | The theorems hold.
