# Implementation decisions Izzy did not choose

Every choice in the apparatus that is mine, one line each, for oversight: what was decided, the alternative, why, and what would show it wrong. Izzy's design, which is not a list of choices but of edges found and removed, each a forced direction (form drifts and meaning holds; no jumps; the artifact a string; sisters sum to one; complex weights; relative counts on rationals; the remap knob off; the rule for numbers) are on the concept page and are not listed here. Added 2026-10-08 at Izzy's ask; every later decision goes here in the same commit that makes it.

## Principles I applied (the fast read)

1. **Exact where the count is, floating where the model is.** The schema runs on `Fraction` (counts, shares, phases); the model runs on Python floats. The mantissa is read as the model's grain, never as the schema's.
2. **The smallest thing that runs, then fix what a round objects to.** Wrong for size (see 14): the size was never derived, only afforded.
3. **One source of truth per reading.** A reader reads either the generator's tables or the artifacts, and says which. Where it reads the tables, the test is an experiment, not reality (see 9).
4. **Nothing reported from a run.** Numbers go to logs; each sweep gets a round written by me before any number is spoken.
5. **Replication by size, not seed** (since round one against sweep 01).
6. **Every piece carries a test that can fail it**, and a gradient check against central differences wherever there is a gradient.

## The decisions

### The constructed drift source (`cocoonml/schema.py`)
7. **A table is a binary grammar**: `nonterminals` with expansions, each a pair of daughters with shares and an order ratio; leaves are forms. Alternative: a flat lexicon with no tree. Why: composition is one binary operation (Izzy); the tree gives structural ambiguity its count. Fails if: the counts the page derives need a different carrier.
8. **Shares and phases on a grid of `resolution` levels**, quantised; amplitudes as pairs of rationals. Alternative: shares unquantised. Why: finite resolution is forced; the grid is the grain. Fails if: the grain chosen hides the moves the step makes (round one against sweep 02 found exactly that once: the step bound below the grain).
9. **The drift's distance is read from the generator's tables** (`delta`, `delta_magnitude`, `incidence_delta` compare two tables), not from the artifacts. The artifact-side reading exists (`estimate`, `estimate_shares`) and is used only for the identification error. This makes the x-axis an experimenter's truth, against Izzy's "reality" design. Alternative: distance from artifacts only. Why: the truth was cheap and exact at first. Fails if: the test is meant as reality; then the axis must be the estimate and the truth only a check. **Flagged for Izzy's decision.**
10. **The step**: at most one grain per node per step; the direction leaned toward the sister that holds a null (the lean as "left alone, resolution falls"); the ends reflect; with a centre and a radius the move is pulled back with probability displacement over radius (mean-reverting); phases jitter a quarter grain. Alternative: the field's parametric shift; or an external signal mapped to steps. Why: superposition of small changes with no absorbing end, the diffusion the page derives. Fails if: the real source's steps have a different shape (the two-source comparison is the check).
11. **The seeded generator is Python's `random`** (Mersenne Twister), seeded per table and per walk. Alternative: a cryptographic generator. Why: replay and speed; the model never sees the seed. Fails if: a model is shown to predict the walk from samples (it would be a result about the generator, not the model).
12. **Remap** replaces a spoken leaf's extension with another of the table's cuts, shares and orders untouched. Alternative: remap on strings. Why: it is the one move that produces a pair training never held, and it leaves the form axis alone. Off unless Izzy turns it on.
13. **Readers**: `n_eff` counts modulus and phase grades per node over nonzero-amplitude readings; `identification_error` is the estimate's distance to the truth with coverage; `identification_excess` subtracts the expected absolute error of a frequency (√(2/π) of the standard error) at the true share and visit count; entropy floors come from the classical joint; `predictive` marginalises over derivations (T1, T2). Alternative readers would be other summaries of the same table. Fails if: a round finds a reader blind to a move (as n_eff was to a moved share: the identification error was added).

### The model (`cocoonml/attention.py`)
14. **Size: width 4, two layers, 1456 parameters** at sweep 05's arguments. Not derived; afforded by pure Python. The derivation on the sweep 05 review puts the match's rank at the table's effective string count (24 strings here), so this size cannot form the full match. **Held wrong since 2026-10-08 15:11; the adequate size is to be derived on the page from the schema's counts and the ensemble.**
15. **Causal softmax attention, no MLP, no layer normalisation, no attention scaling by width**; one head per layer; earlier layers add their attended vector to the stream, the last reads out. Alternative: a full transformer block. Why: the smallest thing with an induction circuit within reach. Fails if: the circuit needs the MLP or the normalisation to form at this scale (the calibration cells are the check).
16. **Embeddings**: token, absolute position, and a within-artifact position (since the last separator); initialisation uniform in ±0.5; later weights drawn after earlier ones so a deeper model shares its first layer with the plain one. Alternative: scaled initialisation. Fails if: the scale is what blocks the circuit. **Its failure condition is met by arithmetic at the derived width (2026-10-09): see 32.**
17. **Token layout, as it was until 2026-10-08 15:48**: the extension token was `BASE + sum(cuts)`, the sum of the derivation's cut indices, with `3 × length` tokens reserved. The sum is not injective across sizes: at sweep 05's table five extensions became three tokens and the meaning a pair carries fell from 2.03 to 1.39 bits, so every meaning curve and control up to and including 05b and the calibration cells read a coarsened target. (My first description of this line, "coded by position", was wrong; corrected from the code.) **Replaced by 27.**
18. **Loss**: softmax cross-entropy composed from primitives on the scalar autograd, scores shifted by their maximum. Fails if: nothing; it is the closed form with its test.
19. **Training**: plain gradient steps at a fixed rate (0.3, then 0.1), batches of four contexts, no momentum, no decay. Alternative: Adam, weight decay. Why: fewer parameters to explain. Fails if: the circuit's formation needs the optimiser (the field found grokking needs decay).
20. **The plateau rule**: training ends when the held-out evaluation fails to improve by 1% of its value over a window of 50 steps, `patience` consecutive times, after `minimum` steps, or at `cap`. Alternative: a fixed step count. Why: a fixed count left every cell still falling; the batch-loss rule stopped at batch noise. Fails if: the held-out readings are too few to see a slow improvement (it stopped sweep 05a after a hundred steps).

### The harness and the run protocol (`cocoonml/harness.py`, `runs/`)
21. **Contexts fill the window**: artifacts drawn from the table until the length is full, separators between, each artifact a string, a separator and its extension token (pairs). Alternative: a fixed number of artifacts per context. Fails if: the count's windows need a fixed artifact count.
22. **The scrambled-pairs control** permutes the extension tokens of a context among themselves; the targets are untouched; the control is the loss difference. Alternative: random extensions. Why: the permutation keeps the marginal and breaks only the pairing.
23. **Curves**: form loss by rank since the last separator; meaning loss at separators by rank; the same-table curves subtracted in the script; only ranks every context reaches. Alternative: raw curves. Why: round one against sweep 03 found the rise with rank was positional.
24. **The probe**: ridge regression in closed form from the last separator's attended vector to the drift magnitude, fit on half the windows, r² on the other half. Alternative: a nonlinear probe. Fails if: the signal is there and not linear.
25. **Cells**: one drift walk per table seed read by three model seeds; the radius swept at a fixed depth past mixing; depth 64; a hundred windows; one process per cell, from a git archive of the branch head; one JSON line per cell. Alternative: more seeds. Why: replication by size (5); seeds only guard against one bad draw.
26. **Smoke before launch**: every sweep script runs once at toy size before its cells.
27. **The extension key is an injective code of the extension vector** (since 2026-10-09 a weight per cut at the grid, `extension_grain`; before, the cut multiset: a count per cut) (`extension_code`: a count per cut index, ranked by size then lexicographically; `extension_vocabulary` sizes the vocabulary from the table's largest extension, 20 tokens at sweep 05's size). Alternative: one token per derivation. Why: a token per multiset is the extension as the page defines it, no more and no less. Fails if: two derivations with the same multiset should count as different meanings (the page says they do not).
28. **Training drew every context from the stationary table; drift entered only at reading.** In `harness.run` and every sweep script, the training batches come from the undrifted table and `drifted` is used only for the readings. This is the design Izzy rejected on 2026-10-08 ("drift present in training; a large corpus carries it") and the regime in which their lodged prediction says the two predictions do not separate ("if the model is trained on deterministic data, there is no difference; the model only tracks the family"). So no sweep so far could bear on their prediction's antecedent. **Flagged, first in importance.** The fix: the training stream is produced along the index's walk up to a training radius; the readings at distances beyond it; the ensemble's numbers (tables, training radius) are Izzy's, the mechanism mine (in progress).
29. **The training stream** (`harness.stream`, used by `runs/sweep6.py`): training contexts are drawn from a table that walks within a training radius around the origin, the number of steps between contexts drawn around `steps_per_context` (a Poisson count: no clock in the index, Izzy; a fixed count was a metronome I had put in); the held-out contexts for the plateau rule come from a walk of their own with the same radius; the readings are taken at radii below and above the training radius from the origin. Alternative: training on a fixed set of pre-drifted tables. Why: the reality design, the stream as artifacts over time. The two numbers, the training radius and the steps per context, are Izzy's; 0 reproduces the stationary training of sweeps 01 to 05.

30. **A leaf's weights sum to one.** The leaf's extension is normalised over the cuts (a gauge per leaf, like sisters per node). Alternative: unnormalised weights. Why: only ratios carry anything (the projective constraint), and one convention is needed for the count of leaf meanings (cuts × weight levels at the grid). Fails if: the extension's total has to carry something a ratio cannot (then the total is a separate reading).
31. **The corpus's rates** (`corpus.produce`): the steps a table takes between artifacts and the switches between tables are Poisson counts around a mean (no clock in the index, Izzy); the next table is drawn uniformly among the others. The means are parameters to sweep, not constants. Alternative: a fixed count (a metronome, struck). Fails if: a corpus's switching has a structure a Poisson count cannot carry (then the rate becomes a reading of the source).

## The port and the first run at the derived size (2026-10-09)

32. **The array model** (`cocoonml/array_attention.py`, `ArrayAttention`): the same architecture as `attention.py` on numpy with the backward in closed form; the scalar model is its oracle (same seed, identical initial weights; loss, gradients and one training step equal to 1e-9; `tests/test_array_attention.py`). Mechanical; written by Gargamel to Claude's brief, the tests run by Claude. **The initialisation scale is derived at the run, and 16's failure condition is met**: with every entry uniform in ±s, the attention scores at the first layer have variance d³s⁸/9 for width d (three embeddings summed, no scaling of the scores), which at s = 0.5 and d = 96 is a standard deviation of about 20: the softmax is saturated at initialisation and its gradient is e^−20, so the circuit cannot form; and the residual stream's variance multiplies by ds²/3 per layer. The one scale with no free constant that keeps the stream's variance constant through a layer is s = √(3/d) (entry variance 1/d, the fan-in rule), under which the scores have variance 9/d at every layer. `ArrayAttention(..., scale=...)` takes it; the default stays 0.5 so the oracle tests and the old sweeps are unchanged; the runs at the derived size pass √(3/w). Alternative: score scaling by 1/√d (decisions 15 excludes it) or normalisation. Fails if: a width-independent scale is shown to train at the derived width (then 16 stands as it was).
33. **The derived size per cell, recounted for graded tables** (the tables sweep 07 draws: `generate(forms=4, cuts=2, nonterminals=3, resolution=4, seed=10·seed + i, graded=True)`). The classical string sets at the origin: seed 1, two tables in play: 94 distinct strings (22 and 74), 30.8 effective, lengths 2 to 6, mean 4.4, suffix depth 6; three tables: 114 distinct, 38.3 effective, depth 6. Seed 2: two tables 50 / 16.4 / depth 6; three 66 / 26.8 / 6. Seed 3: two tables 126 / 21.5 / depth 8; three 195 / 51.4 / 8. (The sweep-05 ungraded table: 24 / 12.4 / depth 4, the page's figures.) So the floor is per cell: w = the union's distinct count rounded up to a multiple of 8, ℓ = the suffix depth + 1; the grading changes the shape (the generator draws the grains after the form, shifting the stream), so the ungraded counts do not carry over. The rule is in `docs/rules.md`.
34. **The first run's ensemble and what it can answer.** Two tables in play, seed 1 (w = 96, ℓ = 7); window length 64 (about eleven whole artifacts, so k runs to about 11 against a suffix depth of 6); the noise axis e as the training radius, two cells, 1 grain and 2 grains (the grid has 4 grains; 0 is the stationary corner and "free" the other, neither run as training); read radii 0, 1, 2, 3 and free, so each cell has readings inside its radius and past it; step rate 1 and switch rate 0.5 per artifact (the smoke's values; parameters to sweep, 31); corpus 12 000 artifacts (about 1 100 windows), read corpora 2 000; cap 6 000 steps in batches of 4, learning rate 0.1, patience 3, minimum 300; replication by size at (144, 8) and (192, 9). **What it answers:** past the training radius, the slope in k of the form excess at small k (zero against positive) and its level at large k against the inside reading (the one prediction as it separated on 2026-10-09 04:30); and the ordering of the two cells by e early and late in training (the training-curve statements of both sides). The ensemble's numbers are the independent variables of the predictions read at two grid points each, not parameters tuned to them; the rates are held at the smoke's values and are the next thing swept. **Pricing, measured on this host (2026-10-09, numpy 1.24, one process):** (96, 7) 207 K parameters, 115 ms per step of four windows, 6 000 steps in 12 minutes; (144, 8) 518 K, 263 ms, 26 minutes; (192, 9) 1.02 M, 514 ms, 51 minutes; the readers' share estimates over the corpus are seconds. Six cells (two radii × three sizes) in parallel on twelve cores: about an hour of wall-clock, set by the largest size. The port's equivalence to the scalar oracle over ten training steps: loss within 9e-16, weights within 2e-16. Fails if: a reading at the two radii cannot be told from the sampling band at k ≈ 11 (then N grows, R3) or the sizes disagree (R4).
35. **The runner's rules are a document first** (`docs/rules.md`): every move of the parameter vector is a row citing a count; the loop holds the same rows and nothing else; the ensemble is not a parameter of the loop. Alternative: a hand-run per iteration. Why: Izzy's protocol, the choices listed and removed one by one; a loop whose moves are all cited has none left to make. Fails if: a cell needs a move no row covers (then the row is written, cited, before the move).
36. **Readers dispatch on the model.** `corpus.position_losses` calls the model's own `position_losses` when it has one (the array model) and otherwise reads the scalar logits as before. `runs/sweep7.py` builds the array model at the derived scale and reads `model.loss` as a float. The scalar model, the oracle, is unchanged.
